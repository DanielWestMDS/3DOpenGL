Inputs:
1 - shadow scene. Soldier casts a shadow onto terrain that updates, but the shape of the shadow is slightly wrong. 
2 - Deferred rendering. 10 point lights with different colours and semi transparent lights. 
3 - Particles. 4 fireworks of random origin that are different colours and fade based on particle lifetime. Press F to cause effect. 
4 - Tessellation. Triangle is tessellated but the texture is slightly wrong. 

P - toggles point lights
L - toggles wireframe view
F - causes firework particle systems to go off. 

up arrow - moves the soldier in the shadow scene in the positive x direction
down arrow - moves the soldier in the shadow scene in the negative x direction
left arrow - moves the soldier in the shadow scene in the positive y direction
right arrow - moves the soldier in the shadow scene in the negative y direction

If you cannot see the tessellation triangle:
you have probably moved the camera behind it. It should be visible if you restart and immediately switch to scene 4.
